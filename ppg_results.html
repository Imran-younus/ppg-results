Comparison — Single Stage vs Chain Per-Stage @ 0.70V

Single Stage — v0.6SB2 (Spice-260604-sandbox) · ULVT · single-cell SPICE · VDD sweep Chain Per-Stage — v0.6SB2 (Spice-260604-sandbox) · SLVT · 101-stage chain · per-stage values as reported by HSPICE
⚠ Stage Delay (~20–30% delta): Both datasets use v0.6SB2 (Spice-260604-sandbox) but different Vt flavors — single-stage is ULVT, chain is SLVT. SLVT has higher Vt and lower leakage than ULVT → slower delay. This Vt difference is the primary cause of the observed delta.
⚠ IDDQ — NOT directly comparable: Single-INV IDDQ = DC leakage of 1 cell (FIND I AT=0 ps). Chain per-stage IDDQ = dynamic current per stage in an oscillating ring — fundamentally different quantities. Additionally, ULVT vs SLVT leakage differ significantly.
✓ Ceff / ACCeff: Comparable — both derived from charge-based extraction. Delta reflects ULVT vs SLVT device sizing and Vt flavor difference.
✓ ACReff: Units differ (single=Ω, chain=KΩ) — shown on separate axes. Ratio reflects ULVT vs SLVT Vt flavor and circuit topology difference.
