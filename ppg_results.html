      Single Stage vs Chain Per-Stage — Topology Comparison③
      ■ Single Stage
      ■ Chain Per-Stage
      Single Stage — isolated single-cell SPICE simulation · VDD sweep · SLVT Chain Per-Stage — 101-stage ring oscillator · per-stage values · SLVT
      ⚠ Stage Delay (~44% higher in chain): Both datasets are SLVT, same model. Delta is topology-driven — the 101-stage ring accumulates wire parasitics and load coupling absent in the isolated single-stage cell.
      ⚠ IDDQ — not comparable: Single-stage = DC static leakage (FIND AT=0 ps). Chain = dynamic switching current per stage. Fundamentally different measurement quantities.
      ⚠ Ceff / ACCeff (~8% lower in chain): Chain per-stage Ceff is slightly lower than single-stage ACCeff — the ring cell drives a weaker net load than the explicit FO3 test load in the isolated cell.
      ⚠ ACReff (~56% higher in chain): Largest delta metric — ring extra wire resistance dominates. The intrinsic device V/I ratio is inflated by interconnect RC. Units unified to KΩ for direct comparison.
